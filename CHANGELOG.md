# Changelog

## 0.1.2 – 2026-11-17

- The sign-in window now appears as soon as Batteri is opened for the first time, instead of
  a moment later.
- The sign-in and Settings windows open in front of other apps.

## 0.1.1 – 2026-11-17

- Batteri now updates itself. It checks for new versions automatically, and you can check
  yourself with Check for Updates… in the menu. Turn automatic checks off in Settings.

## 0.1.0 – 2026-11-17

- First version: a menu bar item that lists every device on your Apple Account with its
  battery level, lowest first.
- Sign-in window with Apple Account, password and verification code, shown on first launch.
- Settings: open at login, lowest level in the menu bar, refresh interval, family devices,
  sign out.
- The engine (the `batteri` command-line tool and its own Python) is bundled in the app.
